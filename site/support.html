Help & support

Stuck, or found something broken? We're here to help.

Email us and a person will reply:

readpanda.support@gmail.com

It helps to mention your iPhone model and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app).

Found a bug? In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Report a bug. You can describe what happened and attach a screenshot, and your app version and device are included for you.

Common questions

Why can't I see some comments?

Comments in a room are pinned to the page they're about. You see a comment once you've read up to that page, so nobody spoils what's ahead. Keep reading and they'll appear.

How do I join a room?

Open the invite link or scan the QR code a member shares, or go to the Rooms tab and enter the room's invite code.

Who can see how far I've read?

Room members, by default. To hide it, go to Settings → Who sees my progress and choose Only me.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Your reading history, buckets and highlights are deleted, rooms you created pass to another member, and your comments stay as "Deleted reader". This can't be undone. If you can't sign in any more, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
