ReadPanda

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

ReadPanda is an app for reading books together. This policy explains what we collect when you use the ReadPanda app, why we need it, who it's shared with, and how you can delete it. ReadPanda is made and run by an independent developer based in India, shown as the developer on ReadPanda's App Store listing. "We" and "us" in this policy mean that developer.

The short version. We collect what the app needs to work: your account, what you read and how far, and what you post in rooms. When you report something, we keep a copy so we can review it. We use crash reports to fix bugs. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your username and email address.
  • Your password, if you sign up with one. We store it only as a secure hash, never as the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: the account identifier they give us. With Apple, we also keep a token so we can revoke ReadPanda's access when you delete your account.
  • The genres you pick as interests.

Your reading

  • The books you open, the page you're on and the furthest page you've reached.
  • Your highlights, and the buckets (lists) you make and the books in them.

Rooms

  • The rooms you create or join, and when you joined.
  • Your comments and replies, which page and passage they're attached to, which comments you've liked, and which you've read.

Reports, blocks and moderation

  • When you report a comment, a reader or a room, we keep: what you reported and whose it is, the room it was in, the reason you chose, any words you added (up to 500 characters), the time, and a copy of the reported comment, room name and description, or username as it was when you reported it.
  • The readers you've blocked.
  • What we did about each report (for example, removed a comment or dismissed the report), and when.
  • Whether your account is suspended.
  • Words in a comment, room name, description or username are checked against a list of slurs and explicit terms when you post. If something is refused, it isn't saved.

Notifications

  • Your notification settings, the notifications we send you, and a push token for your device so we can deliver them. The token is removed when you sign out.

Crash reports and diagnostics

  • When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report so we can fix it. A report includes technical details of the error, your device model, iOS version and app version, the screens you visited just before, and your ReadPanda account ID.
  • Reports don't include your username or email. We've turned off storing IP addresses and location with them.
  • We also measure how quickly screens load, to find slow parts of the app.

Bug reports you send

  • If you use Report a bug, we receive your description, the same technical details as a crash report, and, only if you add them, a screenshot you choose and an email address for our reply.
  • A screenshot shows whatever was on your screen, which might include a book page or other readers' comments. Only attach one you're happy to share.

Purchases

  • If you subscribe to ReadPanda+, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details. We receive whether your subscription is active, through Apple and RevenueCat.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile photo and your reading settings (page turning, page theme) are kept on your device and aren't sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your books, progress, highlights and buckets on your devices.
  • To run rooms: showing members, comments and, unless you turn it off, how far each member has read.
  • To send the notifications you've switched on.
  • To keep rooms safe: to review reports, remove content and suspend accounts that break our terms, to tell the people affected, and to hide the readers you've blocked from you.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs, and to reply when you ask us for help.
  • To unlock ReadPanda+ features if you subscribe.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't build profiles of you for anyone else.

What other readers see

  • Members of a room see your username, your comments and replies in that room, and likes.
  • Members of a room see how far you've read, unless you choose Only me under Settings → Who sees my progress.
  • A comment is only shown to a member once they've read up to its page.

Reports and blocks

  • Reports are seen only by us. The reader or room creator you report is never told who reported them or what you wrote.
  • If we remove a comment or close or delete a room, we tell its author or creator why, using the reason you chose (unless you chose "Something else"), and we tell a deleted room's members that it's gone. We never say who reported it or what you wrote.
  • The readers you block aren't told. They still see you as before; you stop seeing their comments and the open rooms they create, and they're marked "Blocked" in the member lists of rooms you share.
  • The alert we get for a new report says the reason and what was reported (for example, "a comment in Mid club" or a reader's username), never the reported text.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run ReadPanda. They process data for us and aren't allowed to use it for their own purposes.

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Mumbai, India): runs our server.
  • Supabase (Mumbai, India): stores our database.
  • Cloudflare (R2): stores book files and covers.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications.
  • Sentry (United States): receives crash reports, performance data and bug reports.
  • RevenueCat (United States) and Apple: handle ReadPanda+ subscriptions.
  • Apple and Google: handle Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google, if you use them.

Some of these providers are outside India, so your data may be processed in other countries. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Reports are kept after the reported comment, room or account is gone, so we can deal with repeated abuse and answer legal requests. We don't currently delete them on a schedule. A comment you delete is erased, except for the copy kept in any report about it. If you delete your account, reports you made stay but are no longer linked to you. Reports about you stay too, unlinked from your account, though their copy of what you wrote is kept.
  • Your blocks are deleted with your account, or with the account of the reader you blocked.
  • Crash reports, performance data and bug reports are kept by Sentry for a limited time and then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, reading history, buckets, highlights and blocks. Rooms you created pass to the member who joined them earliest (a room with nobody else in it is deleted), and your comments stay in their rooms labelled "Deleted reader", so other members' conversations still make sense. Deletion can't be undone.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a ReadPanda+ subscription; only Apple can do that, under your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.

If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Your choices and rights

  • Hide your reading progress from room members in Settings.
  • Block or unblock a reader from the ⋯ menu on their comment or in a room's member list, and see everyone you've blocked in Settings → Rooms & privacy → Blocked readers.
  • Turn notification types on or off in Settings → Notifications, or turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us. We'll reply as soon as we can.

Children

ReadPanda is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it and its data.

Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections. Sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in the iOS Keychain, and passwords are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
